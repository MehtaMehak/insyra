# Weekly Product Pulse

Report date: 2026-10-15

Scope: findings come from a deliberately selected 300-review sample, not the full 12,680-review set.

## Top 3 themes
1. Hidden brokerage and transaction fees
2. Unclear fee disclosure
3. Customer support inadequacy

## Fee-confusion observation
Fee-related keyword candidates: 206 of 300 sample reviews (severity: high). These are keyword candidates, not confirmed complaints. The majority of negative feedback centers on hidden or poorly explained fees, with users reporting unexpected charges for brokerage, intraday trades, and mutual fund transactions, leading to a perception of deceptive pricing.

## Recommended actions
1. Publish a plain-language fee table before checkout and in order confirmations.
2. Create a support runbook and escalation path for fee, deduction, and withdrawal cases.
3. Review the top three themes with the product team and assign owners.
