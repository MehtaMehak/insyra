# Groww Fee Explainer

Source basis: official Groww pricing and help-center documentation listed below.
Customer sentiment is provided only as review context. Fee-related review language is a set of keyword candidates, not confirmed complaints.

## Why users may see more than a simple brokerage figure

Groww's pricing includes brokerage plus regulatory, statutory, tax, and account-related charges. Some charges are imposed by exchanges or regulators, not by Groww. In addition, brokerage can differ by order type and trading segment.

## Official fee facts

- Equity brokerage is shown as Rs 20 or 0.1% per executed order, whichever is lower, with a minimum of Rs 5.
- DP charges are shown for sell transactions, including depository and Groww components, with a Rs 0 Groww component for sale value below Rs 100 debit value.
- Regulatory and statutory charges include STT, stamp duty, exchange transaction charge, SEBI turnover charge, DP charges, IPFT charge, and related charges.
- GST of 18% applies on brokerage, DP charges, exchange transaction charges, IPFT, SEBI turnover charges, delayed payment charges, DDPI charges, KYC modification request charges, API charges, and auto square-off charges.
- Auto square-off charges are listed at Rs 50 per position for open intraday positions squared off by the system.
- Other charges listed include delayed payment charges of 0.05% per day, Pledge/Unpledge charges of Rs 20 per ISIN, and UPI mandate balance equity brokerage of 1% of order value with no maximum cap.
- Intraday brokerage is Rs 20 or 0.1% of trade value per order, whichever is lower.
- If brokerage per trade is less than Rs 5, the minimum charge is the lower of Rs 5 or 2.5% of trade value.
- Intraday charges listed on the page include STT of 0.025% only on sell orders, stamp duty of 0.003% only on buy orders, exchange transaction charges, SEBI turnover charges, IPFT charge, and GST of 18%.
- A charge of Rs 50 plus GST per position is deducted if open positions are not squared off before 3:20 PM.
- Equity trading fee is either Rs 20 or 0.1% of trade value, whichever is lower, with a minimum charge of Rs 5.
- SEBI guidelines state that brokerage charges for equity delivery trades cannot exceed 2.5% of trade value.
- F&O trading has a flat fee of Rs 20 per order.
- Additional charges such as regulatory fees, statutory charges, and penalties are not from Groww and are imposed by the exchange.

## How to interpret a larger-than-expected charge

- Compare the final charge against the trade type: equity delivery, intraday, F&O, buyback, OFS, MTF, or another segment.
- Separate Groww brokerage from STT, stamp duty, exchange transaction charges, SEBI turnover charges, IPFT charges, DP charges, and GST.
- Check whether an auto square-off, delayed payment, KYC, DP, pledge, courier, or other administrative charge applies.
- Check whether the trade used UPI mandate balance or MTF, since those have different charge rules.
- The brokerage examples in this document are official document excerpts and may change; verify on the linked Groww pages before relying on a fee amount.
